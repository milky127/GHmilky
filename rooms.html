Good to Know

Stay policies

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 2:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can usually be arranged — just ask when you book.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations inside 48 hours are charged for the first night.

ID & payment

A valid ID or passport is required at check-in for every guest. We accept cash (ETB or USD), card, and mobile transfer.
