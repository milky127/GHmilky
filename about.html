Since 2014

Small on purpose.

Milky Guest House started as a six-room house in Bole, built to put up relatives visiting Addis Ababa for weddings and work. Word travelled, and the house grew — three floors, twenty-four rooms, a proper kitchen — but the way it's run hasn't changed much. We're still a front desk that knows your name, not a check-in kiosk.

Most of our guests are back for a second or third stay: business travellers who'd rather not do a big hotel every trip, families in for the season, and a few regulars who just prefer it here. We keep rates honest and rooms clean, and we handle the rest — airport pickup, room service, a coffee ceremony most mornings — so a stay in Addis feels less like logistics.
